## Problem

A food-delivery business needs KPIs it can trust, an early signal for orders likely to be cancelled, and a way for non-technical staff to query the data without writing SQL.

## Data

A **synthetic data generator** produces realistic orders, customers, restaurants and delivery events, including deliberately bad rows, so the pipeline's data-quality handling can be tested.

## Approach

- **Layered DuckDB pipeline:** raw → staging → mart, with tests at each layer and **bad-row quarantine** instead of silently dropping data.
- **Streamlit KPI dashboard** on top of the mart tables.
- **Cancellation prediction model** (scikit-learn) trained on mart features.
- **Read-only Gemini text-to-SQL assistant:** turns plain-English questions into SQL that can only read from the database.
- Packaged with **Docker Compose** so the whole stack runs with one command.
