## Problem

EduPro wanted to understand its learners' demographics and course-enrollment behaviour.

## Data

The **EduPro Users table** (learner roster) supplied for the internship brief. Course, enrollment and transaction tables were **not** supplied.

## Approach

- Built an interactive dashboard that parses the roster CSV, derives age bands from the brief, and recomputes demographic summaries from the active filters.
- Added age-band, gender and free-text search filters, plus **CSV export** of the filtered roster.
- Made a deliberate choice to **not fabricate** course or enrollment metrics. The dashboard states the dataset's coverage limits and keeps the raw gender labels.
